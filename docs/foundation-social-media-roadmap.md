# Rein Protocol Foundation social media roadmap

[简体中文](foundation-social-media-roadmap-zh.md)

**90-day objective.** Help people discover Rein’s work on AI agent safety and give them a reason to participate again. Nori runs the Foundation’s X account. Each post gives readers one useful next step. A developer can inspect a test on GitHub, a nonprofit organizer can bring a question to a workshop, and a returning contributor can explore membership through the Foundation website. The [community roadmap](foundation-community-roadmap.md) covers the wider program. This plan covers social media operations under [product requirement R28](PRD-agent-community-operations.md).

## First month's editorial calendar

1. **When does Nori actually submit a proposal?** Show the three steps from the published rehearsal with test identities. Preparing the proposal wrote nothing, confirmation created one record, and the team verified it through channel history. Invite readers to identify where their organization would want a person involved. [Governance rehearsal, case 4](governance-acceptance-2026-09-28.md#case-verdicts)
2. **What should we test next?** Choose one documented behavior. Open a focused GitHub issue and invite developers to suggest one missing test case.
3. **What do new volunteers keep asking?** Ask nonprofit organizers to contribute one anonymized question for a volunteer FAQ assistant. Use the answers to shape the next demo.
4. **What changed after people responded?** Credit contributions with permission, link the resulting work, and invite one participant to help present the next case.

## Joining AI safety conversations on X

Start with Elon Musk (`@elonmusk`) and Sam Altman (`@sama`). Add “The Real” once the Foundation supplies the exact profile URL. When a researcher or practitioner repeatedly contributes relevant work, add that account to the watchlist too.

Read the original post and anything it quotes before attributing a view to the author. Pick out one point about agent permissions, human oversight, evaluation or accountability. Find one public Rein result that speaks to that point. Queue the original URL, a short reply in the thread’s language and the supporting source. Skip the reply when Rein has nothing specific to add.

Answer the point in the first sentence. Use plain language, a concrete detail and, when useful, one link. Speak in the Foundation’s voice. For posts from watchlist accounts, Nori prepares the reply promptly and the account editor posts it under the original. Nori records the reply URL and checks the conversation on its next pass. [X reply rules](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/x-automation)

**Example.** If a post asks where a human should step in before an agent acts, a reply could read:

> We tried putting the confirmation right before the first write. In our test, Nori could draft a proposal, but it only saved it when the person confirmed. Where would you put that step?

The example draws on a development rehearsal. It does not attribute a statement to anyone on the watchlist. [Published test result](governance-acceptance-2026-09-28.md#case-verdicts)

## Weekly measures and decisions

| Measure | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Reach | Record impressions for original posts and replies where available. Impressions count views and can include repeat viewers. |
| Case visits | Record link clicks or referred visits to the case or event page, naming the metric and source consistently. |
| Outside participants | Count distinct external people who asked a substantive question, contributed a case or attended an event. Track subscriptions and confirmed memberships separately. |
| Returning participants | Count people who participated again on a later day. Include a short example of what brought someone back when known. |

Mark unavailable figures as unknown. When reach grows but visits lag, change the opening or the topic. When visits grow but participation lags, offer something a newcomer can do in ten minutes. When a partner repeatedly brings people back, invite that partner to co-host the next case.

# Rein Protocol Foundation (Rein) Community Roadmap · Version 1

[中文版](foundation-community-roadmap-zh.md)

## Three-month goals

- Grow the community to **1,000–1,400 members** and Rein’s social media accounts to **10,500–16,000 followers**.
- In the final month, have at least 200 members attend an event, complete an exercise or answer someone’s question. Over the quarter, have 10 members help teach, host an event or write course materials.
- Put Nori in charge of daily operations. In each of weeks 9 through 12, Nori should independently complete at least 80% of routine work, including welcoming members, posting updates, organizing events and following up on registrations.
- Submit two grant applications. Budget for four free workshops next quarter and run a public fundraiser once the board approves it.

## Three milestones

| Weeks | What we want to accomplish | Members by the end | Followers by the end |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1–4 | Run the first workshop and find nonprofits and student groups that want to teach with us. | 120–200 | 700–1,500 |
| 5–8 | Teach four free classes. Have a volunteer lead one and invite partners to share what participants made. | 500–700 | 4,300–7,000 |
| 9–12 | Help partners run a class in their own communities and invite members to demonstrate their work at a public event. | 1,000–1,400 | 10,500–16,000 |

For the first workshop, invite a nonprofit to bring ten questions new volunteers often ask, such as “Where do we meet on Saturday?” and “Who should I tell if I have to cancel?” An instructor helps participants build an assistant that answers those questions, and volunteers try it on the spot. Afterward, Nori shares the demo and tutorial on social media and invites people to build one for their own organization at the next workshop.

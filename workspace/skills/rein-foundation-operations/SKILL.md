## People and decisions

Rein supports research, open-source work and community learning around making AI more trustworthy. A contributor can bring a proposal. Nori can help the contributor spell out the goal, time and resources, ask for missing details and show the draft for confirmation. Eligible directors vote; Nori can record the outcome. People retain responsibility for financial decisions and payments. Do not infer board appointments or claim a particular payment occurred.
